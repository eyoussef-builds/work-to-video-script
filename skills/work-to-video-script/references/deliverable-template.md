# Review pack structure

Adapt this structure to the actual audience and available evidence. Suggested filenames are private project outputs, not files to commit automatically.

## script.md — the reviewable story

- Project and scoped completion/version being narrated.
- Narrator identity, language, tone and approximate runtime.
- One-sentence audience promise.
- Evidence coverage and material gaps, stated briefly.

For each scene:

| Approximate time | Spoken script | Screen/action | Shot + claim IDs | Capture state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Estimate after reading aloud | Exact natural narration | What the viewer sees; captions and transitions | Link safe images; keep source mapping private | Historical image / current image / footage needed |

Finish with the user's actual requested call to action, if any. Include captions for later demonstrations or accelerated waits where needed. Keep source paths, private identities and operational notes out of the spoken text and on-screen captions.

## screenshots/index.md — inspectable real images

List each shot with a local link to its safe image, capture type/date, evidence purpose, required redactions and intended scene. Keep originals separately private. Mark missing shots explicitly rather than adding placeholder images that resemble successful captures.

## evidence.md — private claim ledger

Use the columns from the evidence guide. Include contradictory or incomplete proof when relevant and resolve its effect on the script. This file is for production review, not public narration or a public release bundle.

## demo-plan.md — later recording recipe

| Step | Starting state | Exact input/action | Expected visible result | Evidence to capture | Access/side effects | Cleanup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One action per row | Account role/version/sample state | A reproducible action | Observable checkpoint, not a promise | Screen and timing | Read-only, test write, message, paid call, etc. | Owned test artifacts only |

Specify safe test identities/data, prerequisites, relevant platform eligibility/consent, realistic waits, failure handling and pending authorization. A fallback can be a previously verified screenshot labeled with its date, never a fabricated success.

## Review handoff

State what is ready, what must be captured later and which production steps remain outside scope. Link the script and image index. Ask for script feedback when needed, then stop; do not generate voice or video as a side effect.
